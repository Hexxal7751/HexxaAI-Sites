Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the operator of HexxaAI ("we", "us" or "the Bot") handles information when you interact with our Discord application. It covers information received through Discord, information you submit, and information processed by services needed to operate the Bot.

Our privacy commitment

We aim to process only information reasonably necessary to operate, secure and provide the Bot's functionality. We do not sell Discord API data or use it for advertising. Accessing information does not automatically mean that we store it.

1. Information we may process

Depending on the features you use, Discord permissions and the Bot's implementation, information may include:

  • Discord identifiers and profile data: User IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, usernames and other basic profile information made available to the Bot.
  • Interaction data: Commands, questions, message content directed to the Bot and information needed to produce a response.
  • Conversation data: Previous messages or conversation context where a feature retrieves or retains that information.
  • Operational records: Error reports, timestamps, usage events and diagnostic logs generated by the Bot or its hosting infrastructure.
  • Server information: Relevant server settings, roles or channel information where required for a feature and made available through authorized permissions.

The information actually processed depends on the enabled functionality. We do not intentionally request passwords, Discord authentication tokens or unrelated sensitive personal information.

2. How we use information

Where applicable to a feature, information may be used to:

  • Receive commands and provide requested responses.
  • Maintain conversation context or preferences where supported.
  • Maintain reliability, diagnose errors and protect against abuse.
  • Respond to support, access, correction or deletion requests.
  • Meet legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not use Discord API data for unrelated purposes or disclose it for advertising, data brokerage or sale. We do not use message content obtained through Discord APIs to train AI models unless Discord has expressly permitted that use.

3. AI processing and third-party providers

If a feature uses an external AI provider, relevant input and context may be transmitted to that provider to generate a response. The transmitted information depends on the feature and the data included in the request.

The provider may process that information under its own terms, privacy policy and applicable agreements. We will use providers consistently with this policy and applicable requirements. Provider retention and model-training practices depend on the applicable provider, service and configuration.

Hosting, database, monitoring or other service providers may also process information when necessary to operate or secure the Bot. We do not authorize the sale of Discord API data or its use for unrelated advertising.

4. Storage and retention

Information may be processed temporarily in memory or stored in databases, files, logs, backups or provider systems, depending on the Bot's implementation and configuration.

We retain information only for as long as necessary for its stated purpose, subject to applicable legal obligations. We promptly delete Discord API data when retention is no longer necessary for the application's permitted functionality, when required by Discord, when the relevant user requests deletion, or when otherwise required by applicable law. Limited retention may apply where legally required.

Copies held independently by third-party providers may be subject to their own deletion processes and applicable agreements.

5. Data access, correction and deletion requests

To request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information associated with HexxaAI, email hexxal.7751@gmail.com.

Include your Discord user ID if necessary to identify relevant records. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens or unnecessary sensitive information. We may request reasonable verification before processing a request.

We will promptly address valid requests for information under our control, subject to applicable law. If data has been shared with a service provider, we will take appropriate steps to address deletion where required and supported by the applicable arrangement. Data held independently by Discord may require a separate request to Discord.

Removing the Bot or deleting a Discord message does not necessarily remove separate copies previously stored by the Bot or a provider. You can contact us to request deletion of such copies.

6. Sharing and disclosure

We disclose information only as necessary to provide the Bot's functionality through relevant service providers, comply with valid legal requirements, protect rights and security where legally permitted, or when you explicitly direct a disclosure. We do not sell, license or otherwise commercialize Discord API data.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information processed, including appropriate access controls and protection of stored data. No system or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

If a security incident affects personal information, we will take appropriate steps to investigate and mitigate it and provide notifications where required by applicable law or Discord's developer requirements.

8. International processing

Discord, AI providers and hosting services may process information in countries other than your own. Where applicable, legally required safeguards will be used for international transfers.

9. Your choices and rights

You can choose whether to interact with HexxaAI and can stop using it or ask a server administrator to remove it. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to certain processing, or lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. These rights are subject to applicable law.

10. Children and age requirements

HexxaAI is not intended to circumvent Discord's minimum-age rules or applicable age-of-consent requirements. We do not knowingly seek personal information from individuals prohibited from using the Bot under those requirements. If you believe information has been collected inappropriately, contact us.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our features, data practices or legal obligations change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised last-updated date. Where required, we will provide notice or obtain consent for material changes.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, data access requests or deletion requests, contact the HexxaAI operator at hexxal.7751@gmail.com.

HexxaAI is an independent application and is not endorsed by or affiliated with Discord Inc. except to the extent expressly stated by Discord. Discord processes information under its own Privacy Policy.
