Privacy Policy

This policy explains how HexxaAI handles information when you interact with its AI companion features on Discord.

Last updated: 11 October 2026

1. Overview

HexxaAI is an independently developed AI companion bot for Discord. It is designed to process information needed to respond to user commands and provide its supported features.

We aim to minimize data handling on our own side. Information may nevertheless pass through Discord, an AI model provider, and other services needed to operate the bot.

2. Information Processed During Use

Depending on the features you use, information processed to provide a response may include:

  • The text or prompt you submit to HexxaAI.
  • The content required to understand and answer your request.
  • Basic Discord context needed to process a command, such as a server, channel, or user identifier.
  • Technical information handled by Discord or the services used to deliver the response.

The information involved depends on the command, the bot's implementation, and the third-party services required for that feature.

3. No Persistent Data Storage on Our Side

HexxaAI is designed to operate without storing users' prompts or AI-generated responses in its own persistent database or application logs. The information is processed to handle the request and generate a response.

To provide AI functionality, relevant content must be transmitted to the third-party AI model provider. That provider may process or retain submitted content under its own terms, privacy policy, settings, and technical practices.

HexxaAI does not operate or control the AI provider's internal systems and cannot guarantee how that provider independently handles or retains information. The provider's own policies govern its handling of data on its systems.

This statement concerns storage controlled by HexxaAI. It does not mean that Discord or other third-party services necessarily avoid processing or retaining information.

4. How Information Is Used

Information processed through HexxaAI is used as necessary to:

  • Receive and interpret commands or prompts.
  • Send relevant input to an AI provider and return its response.
  • Provide the bot's requested features and maintain its operation.
  • Address technical errors, service reliability, or misuse where applicable.

HexxaAI is not intended to use submitted prompts for unrelated profiling or advertising purposes.

5. Third-Party Services

HexxaAI depends on third-party services, which may include:

  • Discord: the platform through which you interact with the bot.
  • AI model providers: services that process prompts and generate responses.
  • Infrastructure providers: services that may be required to host or deliver the bot.

Each provider operates under its own terms and privacy practices. Its handling of information is governed by the applicable provider policies and settings.

You can review Discord's Privacy Policy for information about Discord's own practices. You should also review the privacy policy of the AI provider used by HexxaAI.

6. AI Responses and Sensitive Information

AI-generated responses may be incorrect, incomplete, or unexpected. Avoid relying on them as the sole source for important decisions.

Do not submit passwords, authentication tokens, financial details, highly sensitive personal information, or any information you are not comfortable sending to the relevant third-party AI provider.

7. Security and Limitations

We aim to minimize the information stored by HexxaAI and to operate the bot responsibly. However, no internet-based service or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information may be processed by Discord, AI providers, and infrastructure services outside the direct control of the HexxaAI project. Their technical systems and policies may differ from ours.

8. Children's Privacy

HexxaAI is intended for people who meet Discord's minimum age requirements and any higher minimum age that applies in their jurisdiction. Do not use HexxaAI if you are not permitted to use Discord under the applicable age rules.

9. International Processing

Discord, AI model providers, and other service providers may process information on systems located in different countries. The location and applicable rules depend on the services involved.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as HexxaAI's features, implementation, or service providers change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.

Please review this page periodically to stay informed about how the service handles information.
